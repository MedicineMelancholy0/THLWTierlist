845:

do the tier list mainly consider actual usage (results) or theoretical usage (calcs)?
for instance mvkasen AoE calcs indicate that it would be atrocious off killer while only having ~50% killer coverage including parapres - yet her results are stellar as she hits killers on way way more than 50% of DS
we also have a million Yang metal content that make nyshi/l0gnako/mvran look like the most broken group of units ever
while exmami has good numbers and better killer coverage but she has no remarkable results other than pairing with a perfectly aligned unit of the DS/EBL
if going by calcs, mvkasen would have a lot of reasons to be lower in EX until NN gives her FR 2.0 with the best anthology card in the game
if going by results, exmami could even be in A because someone like exyuuka has much more results everywhere
As a result, units with a lot of content or no content can be either heavily overrated or underrated depending on tiering philosophy and I would argue the current form of TL indicates that it juggles between both from unit to unit
A more standardised approach to units especially S or above would be much appreciated

The main thing is practicals. And by this i mean calcs that realistically hit minimum damage required for stages. Which is why units like exmami, mv kasen, most of s tier etc. Aren't punished THAT badly for having what is "low damage".
Since they largely actually buff themselves to reach the minimum damage required for content. (I do agree exmami should be lower tho) Nobody cares if you do 2 million or 20 so long as it kills. And for some reason modern units often have a 20 mil cap and then get 1.5 mil on average
because their buff is that shit or they have other in practice issues (like mv ran, who is JUST in practice issues)
Results are then only used to prove/provide counter arguments to this theoritical. We basically take an imagineary stage. See if the unit can farm it realistically. And then tier off that. If im wrong, and the unit is actually proven to farm stages of around that level.
Then the usage proved that the therotical was actually incorrect and they are infact consistent in what was attempted (such as getting x unit to aoe nuke)
When deemed consistent in a facet, wether it be aoe damage or lw damage or utility, it is assumed that you can preform the actions over and over again. So when a character has like an aoe damage problem because you just cant set up their aoe to actually nuke literally ever,
youll see that unit quickly go from SS to A/B tier. As was the case with like cz1.
As such, Ex tier is the tier of being universal in element as well as having universal elements across all content. Whether that be utility (cs2). Universal damage (spriko). A combination of both with some drawbacks (looshi) or everything in the game at once (lootori).
We can rightfully assume that these charaters are ex because theoritical tests and stages proved they meet the required minimums. And then in game usage and testing proved that correct. No its not raw calcs no its not raw usage. Its using usage to prove x is fine/isnt
